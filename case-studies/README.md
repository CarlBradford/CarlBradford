### 📝 **Project Details**

* **Title:** Driving Digital Transformation: How Business Analytics Powers Operational Efficiency and Network Optimization at Converge ICT Solutions
* **Project Type:** Strategic Case Study
* **Tools Used:** Business Analytics, Data Governance Frameworks
* **Scenario:** Converge experienced rapid exponential growth in subscriber data traffic, creating an urgent operational need for higher transmission bandwidth capacity, lower end-to-end latency, and proactive fraud and network anomaly detection across its interconnected enterprise data centers.
* **Summary:** Conducted a comprehensive evaluation on the implementation of Huawei’s DC OptiX 2.0 architecture to streamline data center interconnectivity (DCI), automate live network telemetry, and enhance real-time operational monitoring across key infrastructure hubs.
* **Key Challenges & Solutions:** 
  * *Challenge:* High optical transmission latency and rising bandwidth overhead during peak traffic hours.
  * *Solution:* Successfully resolved by adopting an 800G-per-wavelength optical architecture paired with AI-driven predictive performance monitoring and automated incident detection.
* **What I Learned:** I gained deep insights into how high-capacity optical hardware works in tandem with continuous network telemetry data to maintain enterprise-grade network uptime, optimize operational expenditure, and prevent severe infrastructure bottlenecks.
* **Why It Matters:** Demonstrates how strategic, data-backed infrastructure modernization keeps large-scale telecommunications services fast, resilient, secure, and fully equipped to scale for future digital demand.

---

### 📝 **Project Details**

* **Title:** Mitigating Fraud in Global Payments: The MoneyGram Analytics Transformation
* **Project Type:** Financial Case Study
* **Tools Used:** Predictive & Prescriptive Analytics
* **Scenario:** MoneyGram needed to check increasingly sophisticated, cross-border remittance fraud without introducing excessive security friction or unnecessary payout delays for millions of legitimate global users.
* **Summary:** Analyzed the enterprise deployment of machine learning algorithms for continuous behavioral transaction monitoring, evaluating advanced predictive risk scoring models designed to identify and intercept fraudulent money transfers prior to final payout.
* **Key Challenges & Solutions:** 
  * *Challenge:* Rigid, traditional rule-based fraud systems generated high false-positive rates that frequently disrupted genuine user transactions.
  * *Solution:* Replacing these legacy rules with dynamic, adaptive behavioral profiling models drastically improved overall detection precision while reducing false alarms.
* **What I Learned:** I discovered that effective financial risk management relies on continuously fine-tuning machine learning model thresholds to strike an optimal balance between aggressive fraud mitigation and a frictionless, user-friendly payment experience.
* **Why It Matters:** Protects millions of dollars in daily cross-border transaction volume, significantly mitigates financial risk, and strengthens long-term consumer trust in digital financial ecosystems.

### 📝 **Project Details**

* **Title:** Optimizing Delivery Operations: How Papa Gino’s Scaled Online Fulfillment with VROMO
* **Project Type:** Operational Case Study
* **Tools Used:** Descriptive & Predictive Analytics
* **Scenario:** Heavy order volume during peak meal hours created severe dispatch bottlenecks, extended customer wait times, and placed significant operational strain on store-level driver management.
* **Summary:** Examined the strategic integration of VROMO logistics management software with Toast Point-of-Sale (POS) systems to automate driver assignments, optimize real-time route planning, and seamlessly blend in-house delivery staff with third-party delivery services.
* **Key Challenges & Solutions:** 
  * *Challenge:* Inefficient manual dispatching and poor fleet visibility during rush periods.
  * *Solution:* Resolved by deploying automated dispatch algorithms and dynamic order clustering based on real-time traffic and driver location data.
* **What I Learned:** I saw firsthand how connecting point-of-sale transactional systems directly with intelligent logistics platforms transforms raw order data into immediate operational efficiency and reduced delivery overhead.
* **Why It Matters:** Proves how seamless software integrations and data-driven logistics strategies convert costly operational bottlenecks into highly scalable, predictable, and cost-effective delivery fulfillment channels.
