### 📝 **Project Details**

* **Title:** Amazon Transaction Analysis
* **Project Type:** Data Analysis
* **Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (Text to Columns, LEFT, DATE, VLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, Interactive Slicers)
* **Scenario:** E-commerce operations lacked clarity on seasonal order fluctuations, fulfillment return patterns, and whether regional shipping fees were suppressing order volumes across different territories.
* **Summary:** Cleaned and structured raw Amazon transaction records, designed reference lookup models, and built an interactive Excel dashboard to evaluate monthly trends, fulfillment status, and regional logistics costs.
* **Key Challenges & Solutions:** 
  * *Challenge:* Managing raw text dates, split SKU string identifiers, and column displacement errors across transactional entries.
  * *Solution:* Normalized datasets using `Text to Columns`, extracted core order IDs via `LEFT`, rebuilt standard date objects, and utilized `VLOOKUP` reference tables for automated region mapping.
* **What I Learned:** Regional order volume often hinges on local demand and product availability rather than shipping fees alone, as seen in the Southern region leading total sales despite having the highest average shipping fee.
* **Why It Matters:** Helps sellers optimize inventory planning ahead of Q4 seasonal surges, maintain low return rates, and structure regional distribution without unnecessary shipping cost markdowns.

### 📝 **Project Details**

* **Title:** Empowering E-Commerce Growth: A Thorough Analysis of Sales and Consumer Trends
* **Project Type:** Strategic E-Commerce Analytics Case Study
* **Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning, Numerical Normalization, Pivot Charts)
* **Scenario:** Online retailers struggle with resource allocation due to limited visibility into discount margins, carrier fee disparities, and regional warehouse fulfillment performance.
* **Summary:** Analyzed a global e-commerce dataset spanning 17 attributes to identify revenue drivers, map warehouse logistics output, evaluate shipping provider costs, and measure discount impacts on net margin.
* **Key Challenges & Solutions:** 
  * *Challenge:* Addressing missing customer attributes and evaluating whether heavy discounting actually drives proportional sales gains.
  * *Solution:* Standardized data structures and categorized discount tiers, discovering that steep markdowns (40%+) diminish total return ($90.2M) compared to conservative 0–10% discounts ($110.8M).
* **What I Learned:** Carrier selection directly impacts margins, with UPS offering lower average rates than competitors, while warehouse location strategies in hubs like Amsterdam and London are critical for high-volume fulfillment.
* **Why It Matters:** Enables e-commerce businesses to protect profit margins through targeted dynamic pricing, renegotiate carrier contracts, and optimize inventory distribution across top-performing international markets.
