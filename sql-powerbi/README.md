### 📝 **Project Summary**

* **Title:** A Data-Driven Analysis of PopCrt's Digital Marketing Campaign Performance
* **Project Type:** Business Intelligence
* **Tools Used:** Power BI, Power Query, SQL, DAX (Calculated Measures, Filters)
* **Scenario:** PopCrt invested heavily in ad campaigns but lacked visibility into CTR, demographic revenue contributions, and regional engagement, making budget allocation inefficient.
* **Summary:** Prepared and transformed interaction logs using SQL and Power Query, computed custom DAX metrics, and designed an interactive Power BI dashboard tracking campaign reach, engagement rates, and revenue distributions.
* **Key Challenges & Solutions:** 
  * *Challenge:* Querying complex ad engagement across millions of impressions while isolating demographic revenue segments.
  * *Solution:* Wrote custom SQL queries and created robust DAX measures (using `DIVIDE`, `CALCULATE`, and `TREATAS`) to calculate precise CTRs and female customer revenue shares.
* **What I Learned:** High click engagement does not automatically translate to balanced demographic sales; while electronics and holiday campaigns captured the highest CTR and impressions, female shoppers accounted for under 14% of campaign sales, revealing a clear gap in targeted creative messaging.
* **Why It Matters:** Helps marketing teams stop wasting budget on underperforming categories, optimize ad creative for underrepresented high-potential demographics, and time seasonal campaigns for maximum conversions.

---

### 📝 **Project Summary**

* **Title:** Web-Based Student Publication Platform for *The AXIS* with Integrated Data Analytics
* **Project Type:** Web Analytics & CMS Software Engineering Case Study
* **Tools Used:** PostgreSQL, SQL Aggregations, Chart.js, HTML/CSS, JavaScript
* **Scenario:** *The AXIS* relied on third-party solutions like Facebook and Flipbook, resulting in poor content archiving, lack of reader metrics, and zero visibility into top-performing editorial categories.
* **Summary:** Developed a web publishing platform featuring a built-in Analytics Dashboard that processes PostgreSQL engagement logs using SQL and visualizes traffic patterns, category performance, and author engagement through Chart.js.
* **Key Challenges & Solutions:** 
  * *Challenge:* Providing non-technical editorial staff with immediate, actionable content insights without requiring complex analytical queries.
  * *Solution:* Built an automated, rule-based "Insight Engine" overlay that translates raw traffic trends and timing stats into direct, actionable editorial recommendations.
* **What I Learned:** Content-based recommendation engines and simple descriptive analytics offer immediate operational value for publishing teams, making it easy to identify peak posting windows and high-interest article subjects.
* **Why It Matters:** Transforms student media from guesswork publishing to a data-backed operation, giving editors clear visibility into audience retention, optimal publishing hours, and writer performance.
